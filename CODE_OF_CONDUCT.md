# Code of Conduct

Pramaan OSS follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcome newcomers, accept feedback gracefully, and do not harass anyone. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where you represent the project.

## Reporting

To report a violation, use GitHub's private reporting on the affected repository (Security tab, then "Report a vulnerability") and say it is a conduct report. Reports are handled privately by the maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block accounts that break these rules.
